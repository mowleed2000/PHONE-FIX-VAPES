What's Involved

Over time, every phone battery loses its ability to hold a charge. If your phone is running sluggishly, shutting down unexpectedly at 20%, or draining quickly, a simple battery swap will fix that. We carry high-quality replacement cells for iPhone, Samsung, Pixel and tablets, restoring peak battery lifespan safely in under 30 minutes.

Signs You Need This Repair

  • Battery drains extremely fast during standard use
  • Phone turns off suddenly at low percentage states
  • Battery capacity health readings showing under 80%
  • Device feels hot to touch while charging or in use
